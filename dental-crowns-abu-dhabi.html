What Is a Dental Crown?

A dental crown is a tooth-shaped cap that covers the entire visible portion of a damaged or weakened tooth. It restores the tooth’s shape, size, strength, and appearance. The crown is permanently cemented over the prepared tooth, protecting it from further damage and allowing normal biting and chewing function.

You may need a dental crown when:

  • After root canal treatment — a root-treated tooth becomes brittle and needs a crown to prevent fracture
  • Large filling failure — when a filling is too large to support the remaining tooth structure
  • Cracked or fractured tooth — a crown holds the tooth together and prevents the crack from spreading
  • Cosmetic improvement — to reshape a severely discoloured, misaligned, or worn-down tooth
  • Implant restoration — a crown is placed on top of a dental implant to replace a missing tooth

Crown Materials at Vision Dental

Zirconia Crowns

The strongest crown material available. Ideal for back teeth (molars and premolars) where biting forces are highest. CAD/CAM milled in-house from solid zirconia blocks for a precise fit.

IPS e.max Ceramic

Best aesthetics of any crown material. Made from lithium disilicate glass-ceramic (Ivoclar Vivadent). Ideal for front teeth where a natural, translucent appearance is essential.

Porcelain-Fused-to-Zirconia

Combines the strength of a zirconia core with a hand-layered porcelain exterior. Offers both durability and excellent aesthetics — suitable for both front and back teeth.

Temporary Crowns

Placed while your permanent crown is being fabricated. Protects the prepared tooth and maintains spacing. Made from acrylic or composite resin; replaced at your cementation visit.

Our Crown Process

Assessment & Preparation

Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi examines the tooth, assesses the extent of damage, and discusses material options. The tooth is prepared by removing a thin layer of enamel to create space for the crown.

3Shape Digital Scan

No messy impressions. The 3Shape Trios intraoral scanner captures a precise 3D model of your prepared tooth and surrounding teeth in minutes. This digital file feeds directly into the design software.

CAD/CAM Crown Design

Using computer-aided design software, the crown is digitally sculpted to match your bite, contours, and adjacent teeth. Every contact point and occlusal surface is planned before milling begins.

In-House Milling

The designed crown is milled from a solid block of zirconia or ceramic using our in-house CAD/CAM milling unit. No outsourcing to an external lab — this ensures quality control and faster turnaround.

Fit & Cementation

The finished crown is tried in, checked for fit, bite, and colour match, then permanently cemented. Dr. Emad makes any final adjustments to ensure a comfortable and natural bite.

Why In-House Fabrication Matters

Speed: Same-week turnaround is possible because your crown is designed and milled on-site — no waiting for an external lab to ship it back.

Quality control: Dr. Emad oversees every stage from scan to cementation, ensuring the crown meets his standards before it is placed.

Precision: CAD/CAM technology eliminates the inaccuracies of traditional wax-and-cast methods. The digital workflow produces crowns with margin accuracy measured in microns.

No outsourcing: Your crown never leaves the clinic. This reduces the risk of miscommunication, material mix-ups, or shipping damage.

Crown Aftercare

A well-made and properly maintained dental crown can last 15–20 years or longer. Follow these care guidelines to maximise the lifespan of your crown:

  • Brush twice daily — use a soft-bristled brush and fluoride toothpaste, paying attention to the gum line around the crown
  • Floss daily — slide floss gently around the crown to remove plaque from the margins where the crown meets the tooth
  • Avoid hard foods — do not bite ice, hard candy, or use your crowned tooth to open packaging
  • Wear a night guard — if you grind your teeth (bruxism), a night guard protects both your crowns and natural teeth
  • Regular check-ups — visit Vision Dental every 6 months for professional cleaning and crown inspection

If your crown feels loose, chips, or if you experience sensitivity, contact us promptly. Early intervention can often save the crown without needing a replacement.

Common Questions

Dental Crown FAQs

How long do dental crowns last?
A well-made dental crown typically lasts 15–20 years or longer with proper care. Zirconia crowns can last 25+ years due to their exceptional strength. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, diet, and whether you grind your teeth. Regular check-ups at Vision Dental help detect any issues early.
How much does a dental crown cost in Abu Dhabi?
CAD/CAM dental crowns at Vision Dental start from 575 AED. The final cost depends on the material chosen (zirconia, e.max, or porcelain-fused-to-zirconia) and the complexity of the case. We offer interest-free instalments through Tabby and Tamara. A detailed cost estimate is provided after your consultation.
Is getting a dental crown painful?
The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia, so you will not feel pain during tooth preparation. Some patients experience mild sensitivity for a few days after the crown is placed, which resolves on its own. Over-the-counter pain relief is rarely needed.
Zirconia vs e.max — which crown material is better?
Zirconia is stronger (1,200+ MPa) and ideal for back teeth where biting forces are highest. IPS e.max offers superior aesthetics with natural translucency, making it the best choice for front teeth. Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi will recommend the right material based on tooth location, bite forces, and your aesthetic goals.
How many visits does a dental crown require?
Typically two visits. At the first visit, the tooth is prepared and digitally scanned, and a temporary crown is placed. At the second visit (usually within the same week thanks to in-house milling), the permanent crown is fitted and cemented. Some straightforward cases may be completed in a single extended appointment.
