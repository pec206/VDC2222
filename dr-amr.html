About

30+ Years of
Expert Care

Prof. Amr Labib brings both academic depth and clinical excellence to orthodontics. With over 30 years of experience spanning university teaching and private clinical practice, he has treated thousands of patients across all age groups — from early childhood intervention to complex adult orthodontics.

His approach combines evidence-based treatment planning with a careful eye for facial aesthetics, ensuring that the alignment result integrates naturally with the patient's overall facial balance. At Vision Dental Clinic, Prof. Amr leads all orthodontic cases and is responsible for treatment planning, progress monitoring, and final retention protocols.
