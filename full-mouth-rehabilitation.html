Full Mouth Rehabilitation Abu Dhabi

Comprehensive restorative treatment to rebuild worn, damaged, or missing teeth — planned digitally, executed in phases, and crafted in-house. Led by Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi, PhD Prosthodontics, at Vision Dental Clinic.

What Is Full Mouth Rehabilitation?

Full mouth rehabilitation — also called full mouth reconstruction — is a comprehensive treatment programme that restores the health, function, and appearance of the entire dentition. It addresses multiple problems at once: worn teeth, broken restorations, missing teeth, bite collapse, jaw pain, and compromised aesthetics.

Unlike a purely cosmetic makeover, which focuses on the visible surfaces of front teeth, full mouth rehabilitation reconstructs the structural foundation of the bite. Every tooth is evaluated. The vertical dimension (the height at which upper and lower jaws meet) is re-established if it has been lost, and each restoration is planned to work as part of a single, integrated system.

The goal is a dentition that functions correctly, distributes force evenly, protects the jaw joints, and looks natural — built to last for years with proper maintenance.

When Is Full Mouth Rehabilitation Needed?

Patients who benefit from full mouth rehabilitation typically present with more than one of the following conditions:

  • Severely worn teeth — years of grinding (bruxism), acid erosion, or attrition have shortened the teeth and flattened the biting surfaces, causing loss of vertical dimension
  • Multiple failing restorations — old crowns, bridges, or large fillings that are cracked, leaking, or repeatedly breaking, requiring a coordinated replacement rather than piecemeal repairs
  • Tooth loss from gum disease — advanced periodontal disease has led to the loss of several teeth, shifting of remaining teeth, and bone resorption that must be addressed before rebuilding
  • Traumatic injury — an accident or impact has fractured, displaced, or knocked out multiple teeth, requiring a staged plan to restore form and function
  • Congenital conditions — developmental disorders such as amelogenesis imperfecta, dentinogenesis imperfecta, or ectodermal dysplasia that result in structurally weak or missing teeth from an early age
  • Bite collapse (loss of vertical dimension) — when extensive tooth structure loss causes the jaws to over-close, leading to a shortened facial profile, jaw joint strain, and difficulty eating

The Process at Vision Dental

Full mouth rehabilitation is not a single procedure — it is a planned sequence of treatments delivered over several months. At Vision Dental, the process follows a structured protocol overseen by Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi, our specialist prosthodontist.

Comprehensive Assessment

A full clinical examination of every tooth, the gums, bone levels, jaw joints (TMJ), and bite relationship. We document pain points, functional limitations, and the patient’s goals for the final result.

Digital Records

3Shape intraoral scans capture a precise 3D model of the current dentition. CBCT imaging provides a three-dimensional view of bone volume, root anatomy, and jaw joint position. Digital photographs record the starting condition from every angle.

Diagnosis & Treatment Plan

Dr. Emad analyses all records and formulates a sequenced treatment plan. This includes the target vertical dimension, the type of restoration for each tooth, and the order in which procedures will be carried out. The plan is reviewed by Dr. Hussien Tahoun (Medical Director) for cases requiring implants or surgical input.

Provisional Restorations (Trial Phase)

Before any permanent work is placed, provisional (temporary) restorations are fitted at the planned bite height. The patient wears these for several weeks, allowing the jaw muscles and joints to adapt. Adjustments are made until comfort, function, and aesthetics are confirmed.

Phased Treatment Execution

Treatment is delivered in logical phases — typically starting with any extractions, periodontal therapy, or root canal treatments needed to establish a healthy foundation. Implant placement follows where teeth are missing. Each phase is completed and verified before moving to the next.

Final Restorations (CAD/CAM In-House)

Once the foundation is stable, final restorations are designed digitally and milled or pressed in our in-house CAD/CAM laboratory. Zirconia crowns, bridges, veneers, or implant-supported prosthetics are fabricated with precise fit and natural aesthetics — without outsourcing to an external lab.

Fit, Adjust & Cement

Each restoration is tried in, checked for marginal fit, bite accuracy, and colour match, then permanently cemented or screw-retained. Final occlusal (bite) adjustments ensure even contact across all teeth.

Review & Maintenance

Follow-up appointments at 1 week, 1 month, 3 months, and 6 months after completion. A customised maintenance schedule — including night guards for bruxism patients — is provided to protect the investment long-term.

Treatment Options Used

Full mouth rehabilitation draws on multiple restorative disciplines. The specific combination depends on each patient’s clinical needs:

Zirconia Crowns & Bridges

High-strength monolithic or layered zirconia restorations that cover weakened teeth or span gaps. Milled in-house using CAD/CAM for precise fit and same-day turnaround on individual units.

Dental Implants

Titanium implant fixtures replace missing tooth roots. CBCT-guided placement by Dr. Hussien Tahoun ensures accurate positioning. Implants support single crowns, bridges, or full-arch prosthetics.

Veneers

Ultra-thin porcelain or composite shells bonded to the front surface of teeth. Used in rehabilitation to correct shape, colour, and minor alignment issues on anterior teeth that do not require full-coverage crowns.

Dentures & Overdentures

Removable or implant-retained prosthetics for patients missing most or all teeth in an arch. Overdentures clip onto implants for improved stability and comfort compared to conventional dentures.

How Long Does Full Mouth Rehabilitation Take?

Treatment duration depends on the complexity of the case and the number of procedures involved. Simple cases with no implants may be completed in 3–4 months. Complex cases involving bone grafting, implant integration, and orthodontic alignment can take 9–12 months or longer. The phased approach ensures each stage heals properly before the next begins — Dr. Emad provides a personalised timeline after your assessment.

Multidisciplinary Approach

Full mouth rehabilitation often requires expertise from more than one dental specialty. At Vision Dental, Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi coordinates with a team of in-house specialists to deliver seamless care:

  • Implant surgery — Dr. Hussien Tahoun (Oral & Maxillofacial Surgeon, Medical Director) places implants, performs bone grafting, and manages surgical complications
  • Orthodontics — Dr. Asmaa Butt or Prof. Amr Labib align teeth before restorative work when pre-treatment orthodontics improves the final outcome
  • Endodontics — root canal treatment is completed on any teeth that require it before crowns or bridges are placed, preserving natural tooth structure wherever possible
  • Periodontics — gum health is stabilised before any restorative work begins, ensuring a healthy foundation for crowns, bridges, and implants

This collaborative model means the patient deals with one clinic and one coordinated plan — not multiple referrals across different locations.

Common Questions

Full Mouth Rehabilitation FAQs

How long does full mouth rehabilitation take?
The duration ranges from 3 to 12 months depending on the complexity of your case. Simple cases involving crowns and veneers only may be completed in 3–4 months. Cases requiring implants, bone grafting, or orthodontic pre-treatment take longer because each phase needs time to heal before the next begins. Dr. Emad provides a personalised timeline after your comprehensive assessment.
Is full mouth rehabilitation painful?
Each procedure is carried out under local anaesthesia, so you should not feel pain during treatment. Some procedures, such as implant placement or extractions, may cause mild soreness for a few days afterwards, which is managed with prescribed pain medication. The phased approach means you recover from one stage before starting the next, keeping discomfort to a minimum throughout the process.
How much does full mouth rehabilitation cost in Abu Dhabi?
The cost varies significantly because every case is different. It depends on the number of teeth being treated, the types of restorations used (crowns, implants, veneers, dentures), and whether additional procedures like bone grafting or root canal treatment are needed. After your assessment, Dr. Emad provides a detailed, itemised cost estimate. We offer interest-free instalments through Tabby and Tamara to help manage the investment.
Can I eat normally during treatment?
Yes. Provisional (temporary) restorations are placed early in the process, so you are never left without functional teeth. You can eat a normal diet with provisionals, though we recommend avoiding very hard or sticky foods that could dislodge them. Once final restorations are cemented, you return to a completely unrestricted diet.
How long do the results last?
With proper maintenance, full mouth rehabilitation results can last 15–20 years or more. Zirconia crowns and implant-supported restorations are extremely durable. Longevity depends on oral hygiene, regular dental check-ups, and wearing a night guard if you grind your teeth. Dr. Emad provides a customised maintenance plan to protect your investment.
