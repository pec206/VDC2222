What Affects the Cost?

Prosthodontic pricing depends on several clinical factors unique to your case:

  • Number of units — a single crown costs less than a multi-unit bridge or a full-arch restoration involving 10+ units
  • Material choice — zirconia, lithium disilicate (e.max), and other ceramics differ in cost; Dr. Emad recommends the best material for your specific tooth position and bite forces
  • Case complexity — worn-down dentitions, collapsed bites, or cases requiring significant aesthetic rebuilding involve more planning, time, and materials
  • Additional procedures — root canal treatment, extractions, or gum recontouring may be needed before the restoration phase can begin
  • Implant requirements — if teeth are missing, dental implants may be placed first as foundations for crowns, bridges, or overdentures, adding to the total investment

No Hidden Fees

Your quoted price covers the full restoration journey — consultation, digital scan, CAD/CAM design, in-house fabrication, fitting, and follow-up adjustments. There are no surprise charges after your treatment plan is approved.

What’s Included in the Price

When you begin prosthodontic treatment at Vision Dental, your package covers every step:

Consultation & Assessment

Comprehensive evaluation by Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi, including clinical examination and assessment of your bite, existing restorations, and treatment goals.

Digital Scan

3Shape Trios intraoral scan captures a precise 3D model of your teeth — no messy impressions. The digital file feeds directly into the CAD/CAM design workflow.

CAD/CAM Design

Your restoration is digitally designed on screen using CAD software. Dr. Emad reviews the design for anatomy, occlusion, and aesthetics before milling begins.

In-House Fabrication

The restoration is milled from a solid block of zirconia or lithium disilicate in our on-site CAM unit. No outsourcing — the entire process stays within the clinic for quality control and speed.

Fitting & Adjustments

Dr. Emad tries the restoration in your mouth, checks the fit, bite, colour match, and contacts. Any fine-tuning is done chairside before final cementation.

Follow-Up

A follow-up appointment confirms everything is comfortable and functioning correctly. Minor adjustments after cementation are included at no extra charge.

Cost vs Value

Prosthodontic work is a long-term investment in your oral health, function, and appearance. At Vision Dental, several factors ensure you receive exceptional value for your investment:

  • In-house CAD/CAM lab — restorations are designed and fabricated on-site, eliminating outsourcing delays and middleman costs while maintaining direct quality control
  • PhD-qualified specialist — Dr. Emad El-Din Sharqawi holds a PhD in Prosthodontics, bringing academic-level expertise to every case from single crowns to complex rehabilitations
  • Digital precision — 3Shape scanning and CAD/CAM milling deliver a level of fit and accuracy that traditional hand-poured impressions and manual lab work cannot match
  • No outsourcing delays — because fabrication happens in-house, you spend fewer appointments waiting and get your final restoration faster
  • Durable materials — we use high-strength zirconia and lithium disilicate (e.max) that are proven for longevity, aesthetics, and biocompatibility

Common Questions

Prosthodontics Cost FAQs

How much does a dental crown cost in Abu Dhabi?
A CAD/CAM dental crown at Vision Dental starts from 575 AED. The exact price depends on the material chosen (zirconia or lithium disilicate e.max) and the complexity of the case. A personalised quote is provided after your consultation with Dr. Emad.
Is dental insurance accepted for prosthodontic treatment?
Yes, we accept most major dental insurance plans in the UAE. Our team will verify your coverage for crowns, bridges, and dentures, and handle the pre-authorisation paperwork on your behalf. Contact us with your insurance details for confirmation.
Are payment plans available for prosthodontic work?
Yes. We offer interest-free instalments through Tabby (4 payments) and Tamara (monthly splits), as well as in-house clinic payment plans for larger treatments like full mouth rehabilitation. No credit check is required for Tabby.
Why does full mouth rehabilitation cost vary so much?
Full mouth rehabilitation involves restoring most or all of your teeth. The cost depends on the number of units (crowns, bridges, or implants), the materials selected, whether implants are needed, and any preparatory procedures such as root canals, extractions, or gum treatment. Every case is unique, so a detailed assessment is needed before quoting.
What is included in the crown price?
The crown price includes the consultation, digital intraoral scan, CAD/CAM design, in-house milling and fabrication, fitting, cementation, and follow-up adjustments. There are no hidden charges — the quoted price covers the entire process from start to finish.
Are follow-up adjustments free after crown placement?
Yes. Minor adjustments after cementation — such as bite refinement or polishing — are included in your crown price at no extra charge. If you experience any discomfort after placement, contact the clinic and we will schedule a follow-up with Dr. Emad.
