About

35+ Years of
Expert Care

Dr. Hussien Tahoun has dedicated over 35 years to one belief: that reducing pain and anxiety is as important as the surgery itself. With a PhD in Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery and advanced postgraduate training at the University of Bern in Switzerland, he has performed thousands of complex reconstructive procedures and placed over 5,000 implants throughout his career.

As Medical Director of Vision Dental Clinic, Dr. Hussien sets the clinical standards for every department — from surgical protocols and infection control to digital workflow and patient communication. His philosophy is simple: plan with precision, treat with care, and measure success by the quality of life your patient regains.

Methodology

Clinical Approach

Digital-First Planning

Every procedure begins with a CBCT 3D scan for complete volumetric analysis. Custom surgical guides are designed digitally, ensuring precise implant positioning and predictable outcomes before treatment starts.

Minimally Invasive Surgery

Piezoelectric instruments allow precise bone work while preserving surrounding soft tissue. This approach reduces post-operative discomfort, minimises swelling, and promotes significantly faster healing times.

Medical Director Oversight

Dr. Hussien personally reviews every complex case across all departments. He sets and enforces clinical protocols — from infection control to digital workflows — ensuring consistent quality of care clinic-wide.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How many dental implants has Dr. Hussien placed?
Over 5,000 implants across 35+ years of practice. He uses Straumann® implants and CBCT-guided surgical protocols for every case, ensuring precise placement and predictable long-term outcomes.
Does Dr. Hussien treat patients with dental anxiety?
Yes. He uses STA digital anesthesia for comfortable numbing and offers Nitrous Oxide sedation. His approach focuses on reducing anxiety through clear communication and gentle technique, making treatment virtually painless for anxious patients.
What is Dr. Hussien's role as Medical Director?
He oversees clinical standards across all departments, reviews complex cases from every specialist, sets infection control and digital workflow protocols, and ensures consistent quality of care throughout the clinic.
Can Dr. Hussien perform bone grafting if I don't have enough bone?
Yes. He performs lateral sinus lifts, bone augmentation, and guided bone regeneration to build sufficient bone for implant placement, even in complex cases where other clinics may have declined treatment.
What technology does Dr. Hussien use for implant surgery?
Every case begins with a CBCT 3D scan. Surgical guides ensure precise implant positioning. Osstell ISQ measures implant stability for optimal loading timing, and piezoelectric instruments minimise tissue trauma during bone procedures.
