Porcelain Veneers

Ultra-thin ceramic shells that transform the colour, shape, and alignment of your teeth — designed digitally, milled in our in-house lab, bonded in one appointment.

What Are Porcelain Veneers?

A porcelain veneer is a thin shell of ceramic — typically 0.3 to 0.5mm thick — bonded to the front surface of a tooth. It permanently changes the tooth's colour, shape, size, or alignment without the need for a full crown.

Porcelain veneers are the treatment behind most "smile makeover" and "Hollywood Smile" results you see. They correct a wide range of cosmetic concerns in a single treatment:

  • Discoloured or stained teeth that don't respond to whitening
  • Chipped, cracked, or worn-down teeth
  • Gaps between teeth (diastema)
  • Mildly crooked or uneven teeth
  • Teeth that are too small or too short (microdontia, worn edges)
  • Asymmetric smile line

Which Material Is Right for You?

The choice between E-max and Zirconia depends on your specific case — tooth position, bite forces, and aesthetic goals. For most smile design cases involving the front 6–8 teeth, E-max is the standard choice because of its unmatched translucency. For patients who grind their teeth or need full-arch coverage, Zirconia or multi-layer Zirconia offers the best combination of durability and appearance. Dr. Ammar discusses material options during your consultation.

The Veneer Process

Digital Smile Design

Your face, smile, and teeth are scanned and photographed. A digital mockup of your new smile is created, showing the final result before any tooth is touched. You approve the design, shade, and shape. Learn about DSD →

Tooth Preparation

A thin layer of enamel (0.3–0.5mm for E-max) is removed from the front surface of each tooth. This is done under local anaesthesia. Digital impressions are taken with the 3Shape scanner — no traditional moulds. Temporary veneers are placed to protect the teeth while your permanent veneers are fabricated.

In-House Fabrication

Your veneers are designed in CAD software and milled on our IMES-icore unit using Ivoclar ceramic blocks. Each veneer is hand-finished, stained, and glazed by our in-house technician. Because the lab is inside the clinic, the technician and doctor collaborate directly — no external lab, no miscommunication.

Try-In & Bonding

The veneers are tried on your teeth to verify fit, shade, and aesthetics. Once approved, they are permanently bonded with a light-cured resin cement. Bite is adjusted and the surface is polished. You leave with your final smile.

Who Is a Good Candidate?

Porcelain veneers work best for patients who:

  • Have healthy teeth and gums (active decay or gum disease must be treated first)
  • Have sufficient enamel for bonding (veneers bond to enamel, not dentin)
  • Want to correct cosmetic issues — colour, shape, alignment, gaps
  • Understand that veneers are irreversible (enamel is permanently removed)

Veneers may not be the best option if:

  • You have severe bruxism (teeth grinding) — crowns may be recommended instead
  • The tooth is heavily restored or has little remaining enamel
  • You need significant alignment correction — orthodontics may be more appropriate

Porcelain vs Composite Veneers

Composite veneers are the other option — they're applied directly to the tooth in a single visit using tooth-coloured resin, without lab fabrication. They cost less but also differ in durability and appearance.

Aftercare & Longevity

Porcelain veneers are designed to last 15–20 years with proper care. The ceramic itself does not stain, but the bonding margin can discolour if oral hygiene is neglected.

Common Questions

Porcelain Veneer FAQs

How thin are porcelain veneers?
E-max veneers are typically 0.3–0.5mm thick — about the thickness of a contact lens. This means only a minimal amount of enamel needs to be removed during preparation. Zirconia veneers are slightly thicker at 0.5–0.7mm.
Are porcelain veneers permanent?
The procedure is irreversible — once enamel is removed, the tooth will always need a veneer or crown. However, veneers themselves last 15–20 years and can be replaced when they reach the end of their lifespan. Aftercare guide →
Do porcelain veneers stain?
Glazed porcelain is highly stain-resistant — significantly more so than natural enamel or composite resin. Coffee, tea, and red wine do not stain the ceramic surface itself. However, the bonding cement at the margins can discolour over time if oral hygiene is poor.
Can I get veneers on just a few teeth?
Yes. Many patients get veneers on only the teeth that show when they smile — typically the upper front 6 to 10 teeth. The shade is matched to your existing teeth so they blend naturally. Individual veneers can also be placed on a single chipped or discoloured tooth.
How many appointments does it take?
Typically 2–3 appointments over 2–3 weeks. First: Digital Smile Design and consultation. Second: tooth preparation, scan, and temporary veneers. Third: final bonding. Our in-house lab eliminates the external lab wait, keeping the timeline short.
