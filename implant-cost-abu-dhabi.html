Dental Implant Cost in
Abu Dhabi

A transparent breakdown of dental implant pricing at Vision Dental Clinic — what affects the cost, what's included, and how to plan your treatment budget.

How Much Do Dental Implants Cost?

At Vision Dental Clinic, dental implant treatment starts from 2,600 AED per implant. The final cost depends on the complexity of your case, the number of implants needed, and whether any preparatory procedures are required.

This is not a promotional price — it reflects Straumann® implants, CBCT-guided surgical planning, and prosthetic work produced in our in-house digital lab.

What Affects Implant Cost?

Several clinical factors determine the final price of your implant treatment:

  • Implant system — We use Straumann® SLActive implants exclusively. These are a premium Swiss system with documented long-term success rates, which costs more than generic alternatives but offers predictable outcomes.
  • Bone condition — If you've experienced bone loss, a bone graft or sinus lift may be needed before or during implant placement. This adds to the overall treatment cost.
  • Number of implants — A single tooth replacement requires one implant. Full-arch rehabilitation (All-on-X) uses 4–6 implants per jaw.
  • Crown material — E.max (lithium disilicate) is preferred for front teeth due to its translucency. Zirconia is used for back teeth where strength is critical. Both are milled in our in-house lab.
  • Guided surgery — CBCT-guided implant placement ensures accuracy but requires digital treatment planning and a surgical guide.
  • Immediate loading — In qualifying cases, a temporary crown can be placed the same day as the implant. This protocol has specific clinical requirements.

Why Do Implant Costs Vary?

Dental implant pricing in Abu Dhabi ranges widely — from under 2,000 AED to over 8,000 AED per implant. The difference comes down to three things:

  • Implant brand — Generic implants cost less upfront but lack the long-term clinical documentation of established systems like Straumann®.
  • Lab work — Clinics that outsource lab work to external laboratories have less control over crown quality and fit. Our in-house digital lab produces all prosthetics on-site.
  • Surgical expertise — CBCT-guided surgery and experienced surgical teams add value to the procedure but also to the cost. Dr. Hussien has placed over 5,000 implants across 35+ years.

The lowest-priced implant is not necessarily the most economical choice over time. An implant that integrates well and lasts decades is a better long-term investment than one that may require early replacement.

Pricing FAQs

Does the implant price include the crown?
Yes. Our quoted price includes the Straumann® implant, the abutment, and the final crown — all produced in our in-house lab. There are no hidden costs. If additional procedures like bone grafting are needed, these are discussed and quoted separately before treatment begins.
Can I pay for implants in installments?
Yes. We offer interest-free installment plans through Tabby and Tamara, allowing you to split the cost into manageable payments. For complex cases involving multiple implants, we also offer in-house payment plans tailored to your treatment timeline.
Does insurance cover dental implants in Abu Dhabi?
Some insurance plans provide partial coverage for dental implants. We accept Thiqa, ADNIC, Daman, and other major UAE insurers. Coverage depends on your specific plan and policy terms. Our reception team can verify your benefits before your consultation.
Why are some implants cheaper elsewhere?
Price differences typically reflect the implant brand, the surgeon's experience, and whether the lab work is done in-house or outsourced. We use Straumann® exclusively — a premium system with decades of clinical documentation. Our in-house CAD/CAM lab controls every step of the prosthetic process, and Dr. Hussien brings 35+ years of surgical experience to every case.
How much does bone grafting add to the cost?
Bone grafting cost varies depending on the extent of bone loss and the type of graft material required. The need for bone grafting is determined during your CBCT scan and clinical assessment. If required, the cost is included in your treatment plan before you make any decisions.
Is the consultation fee deducted from treatment?
The consultation fee with Dr. Hussien is 400 AED. This includes a clinical examination, CBCT scan review, and a detailed written treatment plan with itemised costs. The consultation fee is a separate charge for the diagnostic assessment.
