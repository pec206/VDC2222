Digital Smile Design

See your new smile on-screen before any tooth is touched. Our DSD workflow combines facial analysis, 3Shape digital scanning, and computer-aided design to give you a precise preview of your final result.

What Is Digital Smile Design?

Digital Smile Design (DSD) is a computer-aided planning process that lets you preview your new smile before treatment begins. Instead of relying on verbal descriptions or guesswork, your dentist designs the exact shape, size, shade, and alignment of every tooth on screen — mapped to your facial proportions — so you can see and approve the result in advance.

DSD bridges the gap between what the patient imagines and what the dentist delivers. It turns cosmetic dentistry from an unpredictable art into a measurable, repeatable process with a shared visual blueprint.

How DSD Works at Vision Dental

At Vision Dental Clinic, Digital Smile Design is built into every veneer and smile makeover case. The process uses three technologies together:

  • 3Shape Trios intraoral scanner — captures a millimetre-accurate 3D model of your teeth and bite in minutes, with no traditional moulds
  • Facial photography and video analysis — records your lip line, smile width, midline, and facial symmetry so the design respects your unique proportions
  • Digital mockup software — combines the scan and facial data to create a virtual preview of the proposed tooth shapes, sizes, and alignment

The result is a design you can see, evaluate, and adjust before any irreversible work is done.

The DSD Process

Facial Photographs & Video Analysis

We take a series of standardised facial photographs and a short video of your natural smile. These capture your lip dynamics, gum exposure, midline position, and overall facial symmetry — the foundation for a design that looks natural on your face, not just in isolation.

3Shape Intraoral Digital Scan

The 3Shape Trios scanner creates a precise 3D model of your teeth, gums, and bite. This replaces traditional alginate moulds — it is faster, more comfortable, and captures detail down to fractions of a millimetre. The scan feeds directly into the design software.

Digital Mockup Creation

Using the scan and facial data, your dentist designs every tooth individually — shape, width, length, edge position, and alignment — proportioned to your face. Shade options are mapped to the design so you can compare different levels of brightness and translucency.

On-Screen Preview & Patient Approval

You review the proposed smile on a screen during your consultation. Want to see a wider smile? Longer central incisors? A different shade? Adjustments are made in real time until you approve the digital design.

Physical Mockup — Try Your New Smile

Before any permanent work begins, we fabricate a physical mockup — a real, temporary set of veneers placed over your natural teeth so you can see and feel your new smile in person. Look in the mirror, smile, talk, and evaluate the result in real life — not just on a screen. Adjustments can be made an unlimited number of times until you are completely satisfied. Nothing is finalised until you approve both the digital design and the physical trial smile.

Blueprint Sent to In-House Lab

The approved design becomes the digital blueprint for fabrication. It is transferred directly to our in-house CAD/CAM lab, where our technician mills your veneers or restorations from the same file — no interpretation gaps, no external lab handoff, no miscommunication.

DSD vs Traditional Approach

Before DSD, cosmetic dentistry relied on verbal descriptions, wax-ups, and the dentist's intuition. Here is how the two approaches compare:

Traditional Approach

Physical wax-up on a plaster model. Patient sees a rough approximation. Design relies on the technician's interpretation of written instructions. Changes require a new wax-up and extra appointments.

Digital Smile Design

On-screen 3D preview mapped to facial proportions. Patient sees the exact proposed result. Design file transfers digitally to the lab with no interpretation. Adjustments are made instantly during the consultation.

Communication

DSD gives the patient, the dentist, and the lab technician the same visual reference. Everyone works from one digital blueprint — eliminating the "lost in translation" problem that leads to redo appointments.

Predictability

Because the final restoration is milled from the same digital file you approved, the outcome matches the preview. Traditional methods introduce manual variation at every step.

What DSD Can Show You

During your DSD consultation, you can preview and adjust:

  • Tooth shade — compare natural white, bright white, and ultra-white options side by side
  • Tooth shape — rounded, square, or tapered incisal edges; wider or narrower laterals
  • Smile width — how many teeth show when you smile; whether to include premolars
  • Gum line symmetry — identify asymmetry and plan for gum contouring if needed
  • Tooth length and proportions — adjust central-to-lateral ratios for a natural or dramatic look
  • Midline alignment — ensure the centre of the smile aligns with the centre of the face

Physical Mockup: Try Before You Commit

Digital Smile Design does not stop at the screen. At Vision Dental, every smile makeover includes a physical trial smile — a real, wearable mockup placed directly over your natural teeth.

This is not a wax model on a plaster cast. It is a temporary set of veneers that you wear in your mouth, look at in the mirror, and evaluate in natural light. You can smile, talk, and see exactly how the final result will look and feel on your face — before any tooth preparation begins.

Real-Life Preview

See your smile in the mirror, not just on a screen. The mockup sits on your actual teeth so proportions, lip line, and gum visibility are tested in real conditions.

Unlimited Adjustments

Not happy with the length, shape, or alignment? The mockup can be adjusted and re-tried as many times as needed — there is no limit. We refine until you say yes.

Zero Risk

The mockup is temporary and removable. Your natural teeth are untouched at this stage. If you want changes, we make them. If you want to stop, nothing has been done.

Double Confirmation

You approve the digital design on screen AND the physical mockup in your mouth. Two layers of confirmation before any irreversible step — this is how we eliminate surprises.

The Role of the In-House Lab

DSD reaches its full potential when the lab is inside the clinic. At Vision Dental, the same technician who reviews your digital design is the one who mills and finishes your restorations — on-site, using our IMES-icore milling unit and Ivoclar materials.

This means:

  • No digital-to-analogue conversion errors (the lab works from the same file you approved)
  • The technician can consult with your dentist in person at any stage
  • Shade verification happens chairside — not through photographs sent to an external lab
  • Turnaround is faster because there is no shipping or courier delay

DSD Is Included in Every Smile Design Case

At Vision Dental, Digital Smile Design is not an add-on or premium upgrade. It is part of every veneer, Hollywood Smile, and full-arch cosmetic case. If you are getting your smile redesigned, DSD is how we plan it.

Common Questions

Digital Smile Design FAQs

What is Digital Smile Design?
Digital Smile Design is a computer-aided planning process that creates a virtual preview of your new smile before treatment starts. Using facial photographs, video analysis, and a 3Shape intraoral scan, your dentist designs the exact shape, size, and shade of each tooth — mapped to your facial proportions — so you can see and approve the result on screen.
Does DSD cost extra?
At Vision Dental Clinic, Digital Smile Design is included in every veneer and smile makeover case at no additional charge. It is part of our standard planning process, not a premium add-on.
How accurate is the DSD preview?
The preview is highly accurate because the final veneers are milled directly from the approved digital file. Our in-house lab works from the same blueprint you see on screen, eliminating the interpretation gaps that occur when designs are communicated verbally or through external labs.
Can I make changes to the DSD design?
Yes. The consultation is interactive — you can request changes to tooth shape, shade, length, and smile width in real time. Your dentist adjusts the design on screen until you are satisfied. Nothing moves forward without your approval.
How long does the DSD consultation take?
The initial DSD session — photographs, scan, and design review — typically takes 45 to 60 minutes. You leave the appointment having seen and approved a preview of your final result before any treatment begins.
