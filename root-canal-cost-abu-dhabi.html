What Affects the Cost?

Root canal pricing varies from patient to patient. Several clinical factors determine your total treatment cost:

  • Tooth location — anterior teeth (incisors, canines) have fewer canals and are simpler to treat than premolars or molars, which have multiple, often curved canals
  • Number of canals — a front tooth typically has 1 canal while a molar may have 3–4 canals, each requiring individual cleaning, shaping, and filling
  • Retreatment complexity — revising a previously failed root canal is more time-intensive and technically demanding than a first-time treatment, affecting cost
  • Need for post & core — severely weakened teeth may require an internal post and core build-up before receiving a crown, adding to the overall restoration cost
  • Crown type — a crown is strongly recommended after molar root canal treatment to prevent fracture; CAD/CAM ceramic crowns provide the best fit and aesthetics

No Hidden Fees

Your quoted price covers the complete endodontic procedure — from STA anesthesia through canal preparation with the endodontic motor to obturation and temporary filling. Follow-up assessment is included. Crown and post & core are quoted separately so you see every cost before treatment begins.

What’s Included in the Price

When you begin root canal treatment at Vision Dental, your treatment fee covers each stage of the endodontic process:

Consultation & Diagnosis

Comprehensive clinical examination by your dentist, including OPG panoramic imaging to assess the tooth’s root structure, infection extent, and surrounding bone.

STA Digital Anesthesia

Computer-controlled Single Tooth Anesthesia (STA) delivers local anesthetic at a precise, controlled rate — numbing only the target tooth for a virtually pain-free experience.

Canal Treatment Sessions

The infected pulp is removed using an endodontic motor with rotary files for precise canal shaping. Canals are cleaned, disinfected, and sealed with biocompatible filling material (gutta-percha). Simple cases may be completed in one session; complex cases may require two.

Temporary Filling

A temporary filling is placed to seal the tooth between sessions or while awaiting a permanent crown. This protects the treated canals from recontamination.

Follow-Up Assessment

A post-treatment review to confirm healing and check the quality of the root filling. Your dentist will advise on the appropriate crown restoration to protect the tooth long-term.

Why Root Canal Over Extraction?

When a tooth is severely infected, patients often wonder whether extraction is simpler and cheaper. In most cases, saving the natural tooth with root canal treatment is the better clinical and financial decision:

  • Preserve your natural tooth — a root canal removes the infection while keeping your own tooth intact, maintaining natural bite function, sensation, and jawbone stimulation
  • Long-term cost comparison — a root canal plus crown (from 712 AED combined) is significantly less expensive than extraction followed by an implant and crown (from 4,200 AED+), making RCT the more economical choice
  • Shorter treatment timeline — root canal treatment is completed in 1–2 sessions plus crown placement, whereas implant treatment requires 3–6 months of healing before the final restoration
  • No bone loss risk — extraction leads to bone resorption at the site, which may require bone grafting before implant placement — an additional cost and procedure that root canal treatment avoids entirely
  • Adjacent teeth protected — keeping the natural tooth prevents neighbouring teeth from shifting into the gap, avoiding future orthodontic or prosthetic complications

Of course, not every tooth can be saved. When extraction is the only option, Vision Dental offers safe tooth extraction and implant replacement. Your dentist will explain both pathways clearly so you can make an informed decision.

Common Questions

Root Canal Cost FAQs

How much does a root canal cost in Abu Dhabi?
At Vision Dental, root canal treatment starts from 262 AED for an anterior tooth, 350 AED for a premolar, and 450 AED for a molar. The final cost depends on the number of canals and case complexity. A detailed quote is provided after your consultation.
Do I need a crown after root canal treatment?
In most cases, yes — especially for molars and premolars. A root canal removes the nerve and blood supply, making the tooth more brittle over time. A CAD/CAM crown (from 575 AED) protects the tooth from fracture and restores full function. Your dentist will advise whether a crown is essential for your specific tooth.
Is root canal treatment painful?
No. At Vision Dental, we use STA (Single Tooth Anesthesia) computer-controlled delivery, which numbs only the target tooth with precise, slow-rate injection. Most patients report the treatment itself is painless — far more comfortable than the toothache that brought them in.
Does insurance cover root canal treatment?
Most dental insurance plans in the UAE cover root canal treatment as it is classified as a necessary restorative procedure. We accept major insurers and our team will verify your benefits and handle claims on your behalf. Coverage percentages vary by plan.
Why is a molar root canal more expensive than a front tooth?
Molars have 3–4 root canals (sometimes more), each requiring individual cleaning, shaping, and filling. Front teeth typically have just 1 canal. The increased number of canals means more treatment time, more materials, and greater technical complexity — which is reflected in the price.
Is it cheaper to extract a tooth than get a root canal?
Extraction alone is cheaper upfront, but replacing the missing tooth with an implant and crown costs significantly more (from 4,200 AED+) than root canal treatment plus a crown (from 712 AED). Saving the natural tooth is almost always the more cost-effective long-term choice, and it avoids bone loss, shifting teeth, and months of implant healing time.
