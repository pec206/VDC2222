Why Choose Vision Dental?

JAWDA Grade A certified. In-house digital lab. CBCT 3D radiology. Six specialist clinics under one roof. Led by a Medical Director with 35+ years of experience.

What Sets Us Apart

Six Reasons to Choose Vision Dental

JAWDA Grade A Certification

Awarded JAWDA Grade A by TASNEEF (Certificate No. JDC2398) — the highest quality accreditation in Abu Dhabi healthcare. This certification recognises excellence in clinical standards, patient safety, and operational quality.

Certificate No. JDC2398

In-House Digital Lab

Our on-site lab features 3D printing, German milling by IMES-icore, and expert hand finishing. Crowns, veneers, bridges, and implant prosthetics are designed and produced without outsourcing — faster turnaround, better precision.

3D Print + German Milling

3D Radiology & CBCT

Cone-beam computed tomography provides 3D imaging for precise diagnosis, implant planning, and surgical guidance. Every complex case begins with a CBCT scan — no guesswork, no surprises.

3D Imaging

6 Specialist Clinics

Implantology, oral surgery, prosthodontics, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and pediatric care — all under one roof. No referrals needed. Your specialists collaborate on your case in-house.

All Under One Roof

Medical Director Oversight

Every complex case is reviewed by Dr. Hussien Tahoun — Medical Director with 35+ years of experience, 5,000+ implants placed, and internationally trained in Germany, Switzerland, and the UK.

Dr. Hussien Tahoun
