Which Is Right for You?

Invisalign® vs Traditional Braces

Invisalign® Clear Aligners

  • Nearly invisible — no one knows you're in treatment
  • Removable for eating, drinking, and cleaning
  • No brackets, no wires, no mouth ulcers
  • Digital ClinCheck® preview before you start
  • Fewer clinic visits — check-ins every 6–8 weeks
  • Suitable for mild to moderate alignment cases
  • Ideal for adults and working professionals

Traditional Braces

  • Fixed — works 24/7 without patient compliance
  • More effective for complex cases and severe crowding
  • Metal or ceramic (tooth-coloured) options
  • More affordable than clear aligners
  • Suitable for children, teens, and adults
  • Ideal for cases requiring precise tooth movement
  • Excellent long-term results with proper retention

Step by Step

Orthodontic Assessment

Clinical evaluation and digital records including bite analysis, crowding assessment, and facial profile evaluation to determine the ideal treatment approach.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much does Invisalign cost in Abu Dhabi?
Invisalign treatment at Vision Dental starts from 3,500 AED. The total cost depends on case complexity and treatment duration. We offer interest-free installments through Tabby and Tamara.
How long does orthodontic treatment take in Abu Dhabi?
Treatment duration varies: mild cases may take 6-12 months, while complex cases can take 18-24 months. Prof. Amr Labib creates a personalized timeline using 3Shape digital scanning for precise planning.
Braces or Invisalign — which is better?
Both achieve excellent results. Invisalign offers invisible, removable aligners ideal for adults and teens who prefer discretion. Traditional braces may be more effective for severe cases. Prof. Amr Labib will recommend the best option during your consultation.
Does orthodontic treatment hurt?
You may feel mild pressure for 2-3 days after each adjustment or aligner change. This is normal and manageable with over-the-counter pain relief. Modern techniques used by Prof. Amr Labib minimize discomfort significantly.
Can adults get braces or Invisalign in Abu Dhabi?
Absolutely. There is no age limit for orthodontics. Many of our patients are adults seeking treatment for the first time. Invisalign clear aligners are particularly popular with adult patients.
How often do I need to visit during treatment?
Typically every 4-6 weeks for adjustments or to receive new aligners. Each visit is brief, usually 15-30 minutes.
