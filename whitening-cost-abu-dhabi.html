Teeth Whitening Cost in Abu Dhabi

Clear pricing for professional in-office teeth whitening at Vision Dental — Zoom whitening 1,299 AED, Nano whitening 1,099 AED. Interest-free instalments available.

Pricing Overview

Professional teeth whitening at Vision Dental Clinic is performed by Dr. Asmaa Butt, ensuring safe, clinically supervised results. We offer two in-office whitening systems — Philips Zoom and Nano whitening — both completed in a single visit with immediate, visible improvement.

The prices below are fixed for standard in-office sessions. Your dentist may recommend a scaling and polish before whitening to ensure the best possible result. A consultation is always the first step to confirm you are a suitable candidate.

What Affects the Cost?

Teeth whitening pricing is straightforward, but several factors may influence your total investment:

  • Treatment type — Zoom whitening uses Philips LED-accelerated technology and is priced higher than Nano whitening due to the proprietary system and materials involved
  • Number of sessions — most patients achieve their desired shade in a single visit, but heavily stained teeth may benefit from a follow-up session for maximum brightness
  • Initial tooth condition — teeth with significant staining from coffee, tea, smoking, or medication may require additional preparation or a second whitening cycle
  • Pre-treatment needs — scaling and polishing before whitening removes plaque and tartar, allowing the whitening gel to penetrate evenly and deliver the best result

No Hidden Fees

Your quoted whitening price covers the full in-office session including gum protection, whitening gel application, and all light-activation cycles. There are no surprise charges during or after your appointment.

What’s Included in the Price

When you book a professional whitening session at Vision Dental, your appointment covers the complete process:

Consultation & Shade Assessment

Your dentist evaluates your current tooth shade, checks for any contraindications (cavities, gum disease, sensitive teeth), and confirms you are a suitable candidate for whitening.

Shade Matching

A professional shade guide is used to record your starting shade and set a realistic target. Before-and-after comparison ensures you see measurable improvement.

Cleaning & Preparation

If scaling and polish is included, your teeth are professionally cleaned to remove surface stains, plaque, and tartar. Gums and soft tissues are then isolated with a protective barrier.

Whitening Session

Professional-grade whitening gel is applied and activated — Zoom uses Philips LED light acceleration across multiple cycles, while Nano whitening uses nano-particle technology for gentle, effective bleaching.

Aftercare Kit & Instructions

You receive post-whitening care instructions to maintain your results, including dietary guidance for the first 48 hours and sensitivity management tips. Your dentist advises on long-term maintenance.

Cost vs Value

Professional in-office whitening is an investment in your confidence and appearance. Here is how it compares to DIY alternatives:

  • Clinical-grade concentration — in-office whitening gels are significantly stronger than over-the-counter strips or trays, delivering results in one session that DIY products take weeks to approximate
  • Supervised safety — your dentist protects your gums with a barrier and monitors the process, eliminating the risk of chemical burns, uneven whitening, or enamel damage from unsupervised use
  • Predictable, lasting results — professional whitening achieves up to 8 shades brighter in a single visit, with results lasting 12–24 months depending on lifestyle and maintenance
  • Time efficiency — a single 60–90 minute appointment replaces weeks of daily at-home application, making professional whitening the most time-effective option
  • Customised treatment — your dentist selects the right system (Zoom or Nano) based on your tooth sensitivity, staining type, and desired outcome — not a one-size-fits-all approach

Choosing professional whitening at a licensed clinic means safer treatment, better results, and a predictable outcome — all supervised by a qualified dentist.

Common Questions

Whitening Cost FAQs

How much does teeth whitening cost in Abu Dhabi?
At Vision Dental, Zoom whitening (Philips) costs 1,299 AED and Nano whitening costs 1,099 AED. Both are single-visit in-office treatments. A consultation costs 97 AED, and scaling and polish (recommended before whitening) is 195 AED.
What is the difference between Zoom and Nano whitening?
Zoom whitening uses Philips LED light-accelerated technology for maximum whitening power, achieving up to 8 shades brighter. Nano whitening uses nano-particle technology for a gentler approach. Both are completed in a single visit. Your dentist will recommend the best option based on your tooth sensitivity and staining level.
Do I need scaling before teeth whitening?
Scaling and polish is strongly recommended before whitening. Removing plaque and tartar allows the whitening gel to penetrate your teeth evenly, resulting in a more uniform and effective outcome. Your dentist will advise during the consultation.
Do you offer payment plans for teeth whitening?
Yes. We offer interest-free instalments through Tabby and Tamara, as well as in-house payment plans for combined treatments. You can split the cost into manageable payments with zero interest. Our patient coordinator will explain your options during the consultation.
How long do whitening results last?
Professional in-office whitening results typically last 12 to 24 months, depending on your diet and oral hygiene habits. Avoiding heavy staining foods and drinks (coffee, tea, red wine, smoking) and maintaining regular dental cleanings will help prolong your results.
Is professional whitening better than at-home kits?
Yes. Professional whitening uses clinical-grade gel concentrations that are significantly stronger than over-the-counter products. The treatment is supervised by a dentist who protects your gums, monitors the process, and ensures even results. In-office whitening achieves in one session what DIY kits take weeks to approximate, with lower risk of sensitivity or enamel damage.
