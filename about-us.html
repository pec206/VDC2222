Our Story

From One Clinic to a
Multi-Specialty Centre

Vision Dental Clinic was founded in 2015 by a young dentist driven by a clear philosophy: deliver dentistry built on quality, safety, and trust. What began as a single general dental practice in Abu Dhabi has grown into something far beyond the original vision.

Today, Vision Dental Clinic operates as a multi-specialty dental centre housing 6 specialized clinics under one roof. Each clinic is led by experienced specialists covering implants, oral surgery, prosthodontics, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and general care.

The clinic also features an advanced in-house dental laboratory and a dedicated radiology department equipped with CBCT 3D imaging and digital scanning technology — ensuring every diagnosis and treatment plan is precise, predictable, and delivered to international standards.

Guiding Principles

Our Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To be pioneers in modern dentistry by delivering advanced dental care that combines expertise, technology, and international quality standards, creating a treatment experience centered around patient comfort and trust.

Our Mission

Committed to staying up to date with the latest advancements in dentistry by providing the best technologies and modern equipment, along with advanced training programs for our medical team, with the goal of delivering an exceptional dental experience built on quality, safety, and patient comfort.

What Sets Us Apart

Why Choose Vision

In-House Dental Lab

Our advanced in-house laboratory enables same-day adjustments, precise prosthetic fabrication, and full quality control — from zirconia crowns to implant abutments — without outsourcing.

3D Radiology (CBCT)

Dedicated radiology department with CBCT 3D imaging for precise diagnosis, implant planning, surgical guide fabrication, and treatment simulation before any procedure begins.

Digital Workflow

Fully digital treatment planning — intraoral scanning, digital smile design, CAD/CAM prosthetics, and guided surgery. Technology that delivers precision and reduces treatment time.

6 Specialist Clinics

Implantology, oral surgery, prosthodontics, orthodontics, cosmetic dentistry, and general care — all specialist departments under one roof for seamless multidisciplinary treatment.

9000+ Lives Changed

Over nine thousand patients have trusted Vision Dental for their care — from routine check-ups to full-mouth rehabilitations, smile makeovers, and complex surgical cases.
