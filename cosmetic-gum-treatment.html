What Is Cosmetic Gum Treatment?

Reshape Your Gum Line for a
Balanced Smile

Cosmetic gum treatment includes procedures that reshape the gum line to improve the appearance of your smile. Whether you have a gummy smile, uneven gums, or excess tissue covering your teeth, cosmetic gum treatment creates a more proportional, balanced look.

At Vision Dental Clinic, Dr. Hussien Tahoun uses laser-assisted techniques and digital planning to deliver precise, predictable results with minimal discomfort and fast recovery.

Why Choose This Treatment

Benefits of Cosmetic Gum Treatment

Gummy Smile Correction

Reduce excess gum tissue to show more of your natural teeth and achieve a fuller, more confident smile.

Even Gum Line

Symmetrical gum contour for a balanced, proportional smile. Uneven gums can make teeth look different sizes even when they are not.

Better Veneer Results

Gum reshaping before veneers ensures the best possible outcome. The gums frame your veneers, so getting them right first matters.

Minimal Recovery

Most patients return to normal activities within a day. Laser-assisted techniques mean less swelling and faster healing.

Precise Results

Digital planning ensures predictable, symmetrical outcomes. We design your new gum line before treatment begins so you know what to expect.

Confidence Boost

A balanced gum line transforms how your smile looks and feels. Many patients say it is the single change that made the biggest difference.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

What is gum contouring?
A cosmetic procedure that reshapes the gum line by removing or repositioning excess tissue to reveal more tooth surface and create a balanced smile.
Who needs gum contouring?
Anyone who feels their smile shows too much gum, has an uneven gum line, or wants to improve the proportions of their smile before veneers or other cosmetic work.
Can gum contouring fix a gummy smile?
Yes. Gummy smile correction is one of the most common reasons for gum contouring. By removing excess tissue, we expose more of the natural tooth crown for a fuller, more balanced smile.
Should I get gum contouring before veneers?
Often, yes. Reshaping the gums first creates the ideal canvas for veneers, ensuring they look natural and properly proportioned.
Is gum contouring painful?
With STA digital anesthesia, the procedure is virtually painless. Most patients feel only mild pressure during treatment and minimal discomfort afterward.
How long does recovery take?
Most patients return to normal activities within 1-2 days. The gums heal fully within 1-2 weeks. We provide clear aftercare instructions.
Are the results permanent?
Yes. Once gum tissue is removed, it does not grow back. The results are long-lasting.
Will my teeth look bigger after gum contouring?
Your teeth will appear more proportional. If gum tissue was covering part of your teeth, removing it reveals their full natural shape.
Can uneven gums be fixed without surgery?
Minor unevenness can sometimes be addressed with conservative techniques, but most cases require a small procedure to achieve symmetrical results.
Is the consultation fee deducted from treatment?
Yes. If you proceed with treatment, the consultation fee is applied toward the total cost.
