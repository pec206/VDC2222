06 — Landing Pages

Why Separate Landing Pages?

Service Page

Comprehensive — covers everything about the treatment. Navigation, links to other services, blog, doctors. Purpose: educate.

Landing Page

Focused — no navigation, no distractions. One message, one call-to-action. Written in plain language for regular people. Purpose: convert.

Ad Campaign Flow

Patient sees ad → clicks → lands on landing page → books appointment

No distractions = higher conversion rate

10 Landing Pages Built

Implants, Veneers, Orthodontics, Surgery, Root Canal, Prosthodontics, Whitening, Pediatric, Scaling, Restoration

Arabic Language Support

Landing pages support full Arabic — auto-detects visitor device language and switches RTL layout automatically. Separate pages allow independent Arabic content without affecting the main site.

Ready for: Google Ads, Meta Ads, Instagram, and ChatGPT Ads — now available in the UAE. ChatGPT Ads are the future of digital marketing.
