Oral Surgery Cost in Abu Dhabi

Transparent pricing for tooth extractions, wisdom tooth removal, and surgical procedures at Vision Dental. Simple extraction from 179 AED with STA Digital Anaesthesia included.

Pricing Overview

Oral surgery at Vision Dental Clinic is performed by Dr. Hussien Tahoun, specialist oral surgeon with 35+ years of clinical experience. Every procedure includes STA (Single Tooth Anaesthesia) — a computer-controlled system that delivers comfortable, targeted numbing without the discomfort of traditional injections.

The prices below are starting figures. Your exact cost depends on the complexity of the case, the number of teeth involved, and whether additional procedures such as bone grafting are required. A detailed quote is provided after your consultation and OPG panoramic assessment.

What Affects the Cost?

Oral surgery pricing varies between patients. Several clinical factors determine your total cost:

  • Complexity of the case — a simple, fully erupted tooth is straightforward to remove; an impacted wisdom tooth buried in bone requires surgical access, bone removal, and sometimes sectioning of the tooth
  • Type of impaction — wisdom teeth can be vertically, horizontally, mesially, or distally impacted. Horizontal and deeply impacted teeth take longer and cost more
  • Number of teeth — extracting multiple teeth in one session may affect the total; discuss bundled pricing with your surgeon during the consultation
  • Need for bone grafting — if you are planning a dental implant after extraction, socket preservation with bone graft material adds to the cost but protects your jawbone for future treatment
  • Anaesthesia type — STA computer-controlled anaesthesia is included as standard. IV sedation or general anaesthesia for anxious patients or complex cases is quoted separately

No Hidden Fees

Your quoted price includes the consultation, OPG panoramic imaging during consultation, STA Digital Anaesthesia, the extraction procedure, post-operative medications and care instructions, and a follow-up appointment. There are no surprise charges.

What’s Included in the Price

When you book an oral surgery procedure at Vision Dental, your package covers the full journey:

Consultation & Assessment

Comprehensive clinical examination by Dr. Hussien Tahoun, including review of your dental and medical history to plan the safest approach for your procedure.

OPG Panoramic Imaging

A full panoramic radiograph taken during the consultation to assess root anatomy, proximity to nerves, and the position of impacted teeth — essential for safe surgical planning.

STA Digital Anaesthesia

Computer-controlled Single Tooth Anaesthesia delivers a slow, precise injection that numbs only the target area. No painful pressure, no numb lips or tongue — just comfortable, targeted numbing.

The Extraction Procedure

Performed by Dr. Hussien Tahoun using advanced techniques including piezoelectric surgery when indicated — ultrasonic bone cutting that preserves soft tissue and reduces post-operative swelling.

Post-Operative Care

Detailed aftercare instructions, prescribed medications, and guidance on diet, activity, and wound management to ensure smooth healing.

Follow-Up Appointment

A scheduled review visit to check healing, remove sutures if placed, and confirm the extraction site is recovering properly. Included in the procedure cost.

Cost vs Value

Oral surgery is not a place to cut corners. At Vision Dental, your investment comes with distinct clinical advantages:

  • 35+ years of experience — Dr. Hussien Tahoun is a specialist oral surgeon with over three decades of surgical experience, handling everything from routine extractions to complex impacted wisdom teeth
  • STA comfort technology — computer-controlled anaesthesia eliminates the fear and pain of traditional injections, making the experience comfortable from the very first moment
  • Piezoelectric surgery — ultrasonic bone cutting preserves surrounding soft tissue, reduces bleeding, minimises swelling, and accelerates healing compared to conventional surgical instruments
  • Same-day emergency care — dental emergencies such as severe pain, swelling, or trauma are seen on the same day whenever possible, so you are not left waiting in discomfort
  • Implant-ready planning — when an extraction precedes a dental implant, Dr. Hussien plans socket preservation and bone grafting at the extraction stage to avoid a second surgery later

Choosing an experienced oral surgeon with advanced technology reduces the risk of complications, shortens recovery time, and delivers better outcomes — meaning less pain, fewer return visits, and better value for your investment.

Common Questions

Oral Surgery Cost FAQs

How much does a tooth extraction cost in Abu Dhabi?
At Vision Dental, a simple tooth extraction starts from 179 AED. Surgical extractions start from 349 AED for moderate cases and from 549 AED for complex cases. The final cost depends on the tooth's position, root anatomy, and whether surgical access is needed. STA Digital Anaesthesia is included in every extraction.
How much does wisdom tooth removal cost?
Wisdom tooth removal at Vision Dental starts from 179 AED for a simple, fully erupted wisdom tooth. Surgical removal of impacted wisdom teeth ranges from 349 to 549 AED depending on the type of impaction. A panoramic X-ray during the consultation determines the exact complexity and cost.
Is the consultation fee included in the extraction cost?
The initial consultation is 97 AED and includes a clinical examination and OPG panoramic imaging. If you proceed with the extraction, the consultation fee is deducted from your treatment cost.
Do you offer payment plans for oral surgery?
Yes. We offer interest-free instalments through Tabby and Tamara, as well as in-house payment plans for larger procedures. You can split the cost into manageable payments with zero interest. Our patient coordinator will explain your options during the consultation.
Does insurance cover tooth extractions?
Most dental insurance plans in the UAE cover tooth extractions and oral surgery procedures. We accept most major insurers and our team will verify your benefits and handle the pre-authorisation process. Coverage varies by plan, so we recommend asking our team at your consultation.
Why does oral surgery cost vary between patients?
The cost depends on several factors: the type of extraction (simple vs. surgical), the tooth's position and root anatomy, the degree of impaction for wisdom teeth, whether bone grafting is needed for future implant placement, and the number of teeth being extracted. A personalised quote is given after your clinical assessment and panoramic imaging.
