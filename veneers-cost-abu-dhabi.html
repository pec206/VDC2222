What Affects the Cost?

Several factors determine the final price of your veneer treatment:

  • Number of teeth: A single veneer costs less than a full smile makeover covering 16–20 teeth. Most patients treat the upper front 6–10 teeth that show when smiling.
  • Material choice: IPS E-max (lithium disilicate) and Zirconia are both premium ceramics but differ in strength, translucency, and preparation requirements. Multi-layer Zirconia sits between the two. Compare E-max vs Zirconia →
  • Pre-treatment needed: Some patients need whitening (to match remaining teeth to veneer shade), gum contouring (to correct an uneven gum line), or orthodontic alignment before veneers can be placed. These add to the total cost.
  • Case complexity: Severely worn, rotated, or previously restored teeth require more preparation and design time than straightforward cosmetic cases.

What's Included in the Price?

Every veneer treatment at Vision Dental includes the full workflow — there are no surprise add-ons:

Digital Smile Design Preview

Your face, smile, and teeth are scanned and photographed. A digital mockup of your new smile is created so you can see and approve the result before any tooth is touched. Learn about DSD →

Tooth Preparation & Digital Impressions

Minimal enamel removal under local anaesthesia, followed by a 3Shape digital scan — no traditional moulds. Temporary veneers are placed while your permanent ones are fabricated.

In-House Fabrication

Your veneers are designed in CAD software and milled on our IMES-icore unit using Ivoclar ceramic blocks. Each veneer is hand-finished, stained, and glazed by our in-house technician — no external lab, no miscommunication.

Try-In, Bonding & Follow-Up

Veneers are tried on to verify fit and shade, then permanently bonded. Bite is adjusted and polished. A follow-up appointment is included to check your bite and comfort.

Why Costs Vary Between Clinics

Veneer prices in Abu Dhabi range significantly — from budget clinics to premium practices. The difference comes down to:

  • Lab quality: In-house CAD/CAM labs (like ours) use premium Ivoclar materials and allow direct collaboration between doctor and technician. Outsourced labs vary in quality and add communication delays.
  • Material brand: Genuine IPS E-max by Ivoclar costs more than generic lithium disilicate blocks. The brand matters for translucency, strength, and longevity.
  • Doctor experience: A cosmetic dentist who has designed hundreds of smile cases will produce more predictable, natural-looking results. The Digital Smile Design process also adds precision but requires training and technology investment.
  • Technology: 3Shape digital scanners, CAD/CAM milling, and DSD software add cost to the clinic's setup — but they improve accuracy, reduce remakes, and give you a preview before committing.

Common Questions

Veneers Cost FAQs

How much do veneers cost in Abu Dhabi?
At Vision Dental, a single CAD-CAM porcelain veneer starts from 575 AED. A full Smile Design package (16 teeth) starts from 9,700 AED. Composite veneers cost significantly less per tooth. The final price depends on material choice, number of teeth, and case complexity.
Are veneers covered by insurance in the UAE?
Cosmetic veneers are typically not covered by dental insurance in the UAE. Insurance plans generally cover preventive and restorative work only. If veneers are part of a restorative case (e.g., rebuilding structurally damaged teeth), partial coverage may apply — check with your insurer.
Can I pay for veneers in instalments?
Yes. We offer buy-now-pay-later through Tabby (4 interest-free payments) and Tamara (3 equal monthly payments). Custom clinic instalment plans are also available for larger treatments. Ask during your consultation.
Why are veneers cheaper at some clinics?
Price differences come down to material quality (genuine IPS E-max vs generic alternatives), lab setup (in-house CAD/CAM vs outsourced), doctor experience, and technology investment. Lower-priced veneers may use inferior materials or skip the Digital Smile Design process that ensures predictable results.
Is the consultation free?
Book a consultation to receive a personalised assessment and an exact, itemised quote for your case. Call 02 312 2777 or book online to schedule your appointment.
What is the difference between composite and porcelain veneers in terms of cost?
Composite veneers cost significantly less per tooth because they are applied directly in a single visit without lab fabrication. Porcelain veneers cost more but last 15–20 years (vs 5–7 for composite), are more stain-resistant, and offer more natural translucency. Compare composite vs porcelain →
