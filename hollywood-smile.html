Hollywood Smile

A complete smile makeover — 16 to 20 porcelain veneers covering every visible tooth, designed digitally, fabricated in-house, and bonded for a flawless, camera-ready result.

What Is a Hollywood Smile?

A Hollywood Smile is a full-arch veneer smile makeover. Unlike placing veneers on just a few teeth, a Hollywood Smile covers all the teeth that show when you smile — typically 16 to 20 teeth across the upper and lower arches. The goal is a complete transformation: uniform colour, symmetrical shape, and a harmonious smile line from corner to corner.

The term "Hollywood Smile" comes from the bright, even smiles associated with celebrities and public figures. But a modern Hollywood Smile is not one-size-fits-all — it is a customised treatment designed around your facial proportions, lip line, skin tone, and personal preferences.

At Vision Dental Clinic, every Hollywood Smile case begins with Digital Smile Design (DSD) — you see a realistic preview of your final result before any tooth is touched.

Natural vs Bright White: Your Choice

One of the biggest decisions in a Hollywood Smile is the shade. There are two broad directions:

  • Bright white (the "Hollywood" look) — a very white, uniform shade that stands out against the skin. Popular among patients who want a bold, high-contrast smile. Works well with darker skin tones
  • Natural white — a lighter shade than your original teeth, but with subtle colour variation and translucency that mimics natural enamel. Most patients cannot tell these are veneers

Neither option is "better" — it depends entirely on what you want. During your DSD consultation, Dr. Ammar shows you both options digitally on your own face so you can compare before committing.

Realistic Expectations

A Hollywood Smile can dramatically change the colour, shape, alignment, and symmetry of your smile. However, it does not change your jaw structure, lip shape, or gum line on its own. Patients with a "gummy smile" may need gum recontouring first. Patients with significant bite issues may need orthodontics before veneers. Dr. Ammar assesses all of this during the initial consultation.

What's Included in a Hollywood Smile at Vision Dental

A Hollywood Smile at our clinic is not just "20 veneers glued on." It is a planned, multi-step treatment that includes:

  • Digital Smile Design consultation — facial analysis, smile simulation, and shade selection using DSD software
  • Diagnostic wax-up or digital mockup — a trial version of your smile so you can preview and approve the result
  • Tooth preparation — minimal enamel reduction (0.3–0.5mm for E-max) under local anaesthesia
  • 3Shape digital impressions — no traditional moulds, fully digital scanning
  • In-house CAD/CAM fabrication — veneers are designed and milled in our on-site lab using Ivoclar materials and IMES-icore milling
  • Try-in and adjustments — veneers are tested on your teeth before permanent bonding
  • Final bonding and finishing — permanent placement, bite adjustment, and polishing
  • Follow-up appointment — post-bonding check to ensure fit, bite, and comfort

Who Is a Good Candidate?

A Hollywood Smile works best for patients who:

  • Have healthy gums and no active periodontal disease
  • Have sufficient enamel remaining for bonding (veneers adhere to enamel, not dentin)
  • Want a full-smile transformation — not just a few teeth
  • Have realistic expectations about what veneers can and cannot change
  • Understand the treatment is irreversible — enamel is permanently reduced

A Hollywood Smile may not be the right option if you have severe bruxism (grinding), significant jaw misalignment, or untreated gum disease. Dr. Ammar evaluates candidacy during the initial DSD consultation.

The Hollywood Smile Process

Digital Smile Design Consultation

Your face, smile, and teeth are photographed and scanned. Using Digital Smile Design software, Dr. Ammar creates a simulation of your Hollywood Smile — showing exact shape, shade, and proportions. You approve the design before any work begins.

Mock-Up Trial

A temporary mock-up of your new smile is placed over your teeth so you can see and feel the result in real life — not just on screen. Adjustments to shape or length are made at this stage.

Tooth Preparation & Digital Scan

A thin layer of enamel (0.3–0.5mm) is removed from each tooth under local anaesthesia. A 3Shape digital scan replaces traditional moulds. Temporary veneers are placed to protect your teeth while the permanent set is fabricated.

In-House Fabrication

Your veneers are designed in CAD software and milled on our IMES-icore unit. Each veneer is hand-finished, stained, and glazed by our in-house technician. Because the lab is inside the clinic, the technician and dentist collaborate directly — no external lab delays or miscommunication.

Try-In, Bonding & Follow-Up

The veneers are tried on to verify fit, shade, and aesthetics. Once approved, they are permanently bonded with light-cured resin cement, bite is adjusted, and surfaces are polished. A follow-up appointment ensures everything settles correctly.

Hollywood Smile vs Individual Veneers

The key difference is scope. Individual veneers treat specific teeth — a chipped tooth, a gap, a single discolouration. A Hollywood Smile treats the entire visible smile as a single design project.

  • Individual veneers (2–8 teeth): shade-matched to your existing teeth. The goal is to blend in
  • Hollywood Smile (16–20 teeth): all visible teeth are covered, so you can choose any shade — there are no existing teeth to match against. The goal is a complete transformation

Because a Hollywood Smile covers the entire arch, it also corrects minor alignment, spacing, and symmetry issues that individual veneers cannot address.

Aftercare & Longevity

A well-maintained Hollywood Smile lasts 15–20 years or more. The porcelain does not stain, but the bonding margins require consistent oral hygiene. Night guards are recommended for patients with any grinding tendency.

Common Questions

Hollywood Smile FAQs

How many teeth are included in a Hollywood Smile?
A Hollywood Smile typically covers 16 to 20 teeth — all the teeth visible when you smile. This usually includes the upper and lower arches from premolar to premolar. The exact number depends on your smile width and lip line.
How long does a Hollywood Smile take?
Typically 2 to 3 weeks from the first preparation appointment to final bonding. The DSD consultation happens before that. Our in-house lab eliminates external lab delays, keeping the timeline short — which is especially important for international patients visiting Abu Dhabi.
Does a Hollywood Smile look fake?
Only if that is what you want. A Hollywood Smile can range from ultra-bright (the classic "Hollywood" look) to a very natural shade with subtle translucency. With Digital Smile Design, you choose the exact shade and shape — and you preview it on-screen before any work begins.
What is the cost of a Hollywood Smile in Abu Dhabi?
A Hollywood Smile at Vision Dental starts from AED 9,700 for 16 teeth. The final cost depends on the number of teeth and the material chosen (E-max, Zirconia, or multi-layer Zirconia). A detailed quote is provided after the DSD consultation. See full pricing →
Can I fly back home the same week I get my Hollywood Smile?
Yes. Once the veneers are bonded, you can resume normal activities immediately — including travel. We schedule a follow-up before your departure to check the bite and bonding. Many international patients complete their Hollywood Smile during a 2–3 week visit to Abu Dhabi. Medical tourism info →
