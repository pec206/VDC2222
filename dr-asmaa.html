About

10+ Years of
Expert Care

Dr. Asmaa Butt is a certified Invisalign® provider and cosmetic dentist with over 10 years of clinical experience. As one of Vision Dental Clinic's primary providers of clear aligner therapy, she manages Invisalign® cases from initial consultation through ClinCheck® digital planning to final retention.

Known for her calm, reassuring consultation style and her meticulous attention to aesthetic outcomes, Dr. Asmaa combines Invisalign® treatment with cosmetic procedures — including smile design, whitening, and bonding — to deliver comprehensive smile transformations. She also manages international Invisalign® patients with remote monitoring support.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Dr. Asmaa the Invisalign provider at Vision Dental?
Yes. Dr. Asmaa Butt is the clinic's certified Invisalign provider. She manages all clear aligner cases, from initial consultation and 3D scanning to treatment monitoring and final refinements.
Can Dr. Asmaa treat my child?
Yes. She provides comprehensive pediatric dental care including check-ups, fluoride treatments, fissure sealants, restorations, and space maintainers. She uses child-friendly techniques with STA anesthesia and Nitrous Oxide sedation when needed.
Which whitening treatment does Dr. Asmaa recommend?
She offers both Philips Zoom (1,299 AED) and Nano whitening (1,099 AED). Zoom delivers faster, more dramatic results in about 45 minutes. The best option depends on your sensitivity level and desired shade improvement — she'll recommend during consultation.
How long does Invisalign treatment take?
Most cases take 6–18 months depending on complexity. Simple alignment cases may be completed in as few as 6 months, while more complex bite corrections take longer. A treatment timeline is provided after the initial scan.
Does whitening damage tooth enamel?
Professional whitening under dental supervision is clinically safe for enamel when applied correctly. Dr. Asmaa customises the treatment to your sensitivity level and provides aftercare instructions to maintain results.
