What Is Smile Design?

See Your New Smile
Before We Begin

Digital Smile Design (DSD) is a planning technology that matches your smile to your unique facial structure — analyzing your face shape, lip line, and tooth proportions to create a smile that looks naturally yours. You preview the result on-screen before any tooth is touched.

Porcelain veneers are ultra-thin ceramic shells bonded to the front surface of your teeth. At Vision Dental Clinic, every veneer is designed with CAD/CAM software and milled in our in-house lab. Material options include Zirconia, Multi-layer Zirconia, and IPS E-max — each selected based on your case for the best balance of strength, translucency, and natural appearance.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much does a smile makeover cost in Abu Dhabi?
Smile design packages at Vision Dental start from 9,700 AED. The price includes Digital Smile Design preview, preparation, and final placement. The exact cost depends on the number of teeth, material choice, and case complexity. We use premium Ivoclar materials crafted in our in-house CAD/CAM laboratory.
How long do porcelain veneers last?
With proper care, porcelain veneers typically last 15-20 years. Our in-house lab uses Ivoclar materials and German IMES-icore milling for exceptional durability and aesthetics.
What is Digital Smile Design?
Digital Smile Design (DSD) allows you to see a preview of your new smile before any treatment begins. Using our 3Shape scanner, we create a digital mockup that you can review and adjust until you're completely satisfied.
Do veneers damage your teeth?
Modern veneer preparation is minimally invasive — only 0.3-0.5mm of enamel is removed. Our digital workflow ensures the most conservative preparation possible while achieving a natural, beautiful result.
Can I choose my veneer shade?
Yes. During the Digital Smile Design process, you select the shade, shape, and size of your veneers. We show you the result digitally first, and you approve before any preparation begins.
How many visits does a smile makeover take?
Typically 2-3 visits over 1-2 weeks. First visit: consultation and Digital Smile Design. Second visit: preparation and temporary veneers. Third visit: final placement of your permanent veneers.
