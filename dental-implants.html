What Are Dental Implants?

The Gold Standard for
Replacing Missing Teeth

A dental implant is a small titanium post surgically placed into the jawbone to replace the root of a missing tooth. Over time, it fuses with the bone through a process called osseointegration — creating a permanent, stable foundation for a crown, bridge, or denture.

At Vision Dental Clinic, we use Straumann® SLActive implants — the world's leading implant brand — combined with CBCT 3D imaging and guided surgery to ensure every implant is placed with precision and predictability.

Why Choose Implants

Why Implants Are the Smartest Choice

Look & Feel Natural

Implants look, feel, and function exactly like natural teeth. No one can tell the difference — not even you.

Lifetime Durability

With proper oral hygiene, Straumann® implants are designed to last a lifetime — no replacements, no compromises.

Preserve Jawbone

Unlike bridges or dentures, implants stimulate the jawbone and prevent the bone loss that follows tooth loss.

No Adjacent Damage

Traditional bridges require grinding down healthy teeth. Implants stand alone — neighbouring teeth stay untouched.

Eat Anything

Bite into an apple. Enjoy a steak. Implants restore full chewing function so you can eat without restriction or anxiety.

Swiss Precision Technology

Straumann® Roxolid implants combine titanium and zirconia for maximum strength with minimum bone removal.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Are dental implants painful?
The procedure is performed under local anaesthesia, so you feel no pain during surgery. Most patients describe mild soreness for 2–3 days afterward, which is well managed with standard pain relief. Dr. Hussien's guided surgery technique is minimally invasive, meaning less trauma and faster recovery.
How long does the whole treatment take?
A single implant with a crown typically takes 3–4 months from surgery to final restoration. For cases with immediate loading (same-day teeth), the functional result can be achieved in 1–2 visits. Your exact timeline depends on bone density and the complexity of your case, which is assessed during your CBCT consultation.
Am I a suitable candidate for implants?
Most adults with good general health are suitable candidates. Adequate jawbone density is important, but even patients with bone loss may still qualify — bone grafting or sinus lift procedures can prepare the site. Uncontrolled diabetes or active smoking may affect healing. A full assessment including a CBCT scan gives a definitive answer for your specific case.
Why Straumann® specifically?
Straumann® is the world's most clinically documented implant system — used in over 100 countries with decades of peer-reviewed research supporting their outcomes. Their SLActive surface technology achieves osseointegration up to 40% faster than standard implants. Dr. Hussien personally visited Straumann's Switzerland headquarters before making them the exclusive choice at Vision Dental Clinic.
How long do dental implants last?
With proper oral hygiene and regular dental check-ups, Straumann® implants are designed to last a lifetime. Clinical studies show a 98% success rate at 10 years. The crown attached to the implant may require replacement after 15–20 years with normal wear, but the implant post itself remains permanently integrated in the jawbone.
What is All-on-X and who is it for?
All-on-4 or All-on-6 (All-on-X) is a full-arch solution where an entire jaw of teeth is supported by just 4 to 6 strategically placed implants. It's designed for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth, or those with severely failing dentitions. Same-day loading is often possible, meaning you leave with functional teeth on the day of surgery.
