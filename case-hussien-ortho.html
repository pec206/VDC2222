Orthodontic Treatment & Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Dr. Hussien Tahoun

Surgical removal of four wisdom teeth during an ongoing orthodontic treatment plan — coordinated between Prof. Amr Labib and Dr. Hussien to ensure seamless care.

Clinical images for this case are not available for publication at this time.

Patient Concern

Ongoing Orthodontic Treatment

The patient was undergoing orthodontic treatment that had originally been started at another dental facility and was subsequently continued under the care of Prof. Amr Labib at Vision Dental Clinic. As part of the overall orthodontic plan, the wisdom teeth needed to be removed to allow proper tooth alignment.

Clinical Challenge

Coordinating Surgery with Active Orthodontics

The challenge was performing the surgical removal of all four wisdom teeth while the patient was in the middle of active orthodontic treatment. The timing and approach had to be carefully coordinated to avoid disrupting the orthodontic progress while addressing the surgical need.

Treatment Approach

Specialist Referral & Coordination

Prof. Amr Labib referred the patient to Dr. Hussien for the surgical removal of the four wisdom teeth. The procedure was planned in coordination with the ongoing orthodontic treatment to ensure both the surgical and orthodontic outcomes were optimized.

Result

Seamless Surgical & Orthodontic Care

All four wisdom teeth were successfully removed, allowing the orthodontic treatment to continue without interruption. This case demonstrates the importance of coordination between orthodontic and surgical care when extractions are required during an ongoing treatment plan.
