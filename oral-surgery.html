What Is Oral & Maxillofacial Surgery?

Surgical Excellence

Our Surgical Capabilities

What We Treat & Fix

Wisdom Tooth Extraction

Simple and complex wisdom tooth removals — including impacted, angled, or deeply embedded teeth. CBCT imaging ensures the safest surgical approach, especially near nerve canals.

Bone Grafting

Where bone volume is insufficient for implants, bone grafting rebuilds the jaw. We use autogenous, xenograft, and synthetic grafting materials depending on your specific case.

Sinus Lift

When upper jaw bone is too thin for implants, a sinus augmentation adds bone height in the posterior maxilla. Both lateral window and crestal approaches are performed at our clinic.

Exposure of Impacted Teeth

Impacted canines or other unerupted teeth are surgically exposed to allow orthodontic guidance into the correct position.

Surgical Extractions

Retained roots, fractured teeth, teeth with curved roots, or those near critical anatomy require surgical rather than simple extraction — performed under full anaesthesia control.

Full-Arch Surgery (All-on-X)

Full mouth reconstruction combining multiple extractions, bone management, and immediate implant placement for complete arch rehabilitation in a single surgical session.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is oral surgery painful?
Surgery is performed under local anaesthesia — you feel no pain during the procedure. Mild soreness for 2–5 days afterward is normal and managed with prescribed medication. Dr. Hussien's minimally invasive techniques reduce post-operative discomfort significantly compared to conventional approaches.
How long does recovery take?
Recovery depends on the procedure. Simple extractions: 1–3 days. Wisdom teeth removal: 3–5 days. Bone grafting: 7–14 days of reduced activity. Sinus lift: 10–14 days. Most patients return to desk work within 1–2 days and strenuous activity after 7–10 days. Specific instructions are given for every procedure.
When can I get an implant after bone grafting?
After bone grafting, a healing period of 4–6 months is typically required before implant placement — allowing the graft to integrate fully with the existing bone. In some cases, simultaneous grafting and implant placement is possible. Dr. Hussien determines the optimal sequence based on your CBCT imaging.
Do I need a referral for oral surgery?
No referral is needed. You can book directly with our clinic. Dr. Hussien's team will review your history, take a CBCT scan, and determine the most appropriate treatment approach at your first consultation.
What is the STA system?
STA (Single Tooth Anaesthesia) is a computer-controlled local anaesthesia delivery system. It administers the anaesthetic at a precise, controlled flow rate — significantly reducing injection discomfort compared to conventional syringes. Most patients describe the experience as a gentle pressure rather than a sting.
